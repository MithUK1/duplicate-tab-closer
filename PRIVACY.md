# Duplicate Tab Closer: Privacy Policy

Duplicate Tab Closer is a browser extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox. It is made by MithUK1 as an individual developer. This policy covers the extension only.

## Summary

The extension does not collect, send, sell or share any data. Everything it works with stays inside your browser on your computer. It has no server, no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it makes no network requests.

## Data the extension handles

| Data | Why | Where it is kept | How long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Web addresses (URLs) and titles of your open tabs | To work out which tabs are duplicates, show the count on the icon and close duplicates | In the browser's memory while the browser runs | Only while the tab is open |
| URLs, titles and site icon addresses of tabs the extension closed | To let you reopen a closed tab (Undo and the history page) | In memory only. If you turn on "Keep the history after the browser closes", in the browser's extension storage on your computer | 5 minutes by default (you can change it), then removed. Wiped when the browser closes unless you chose to keep it. You can clear it at any time |
| Your settings (options and the list of sites to never touch) | To remember your choices | The browser's own extension storage on your computer | Until you reset them or remove the extension |

The extension does not read page content, form data, cookies, passwords, browsing history or anything you type. It only sees tab addresses and titles supplied by the browser.

## Collection, use and sharing

- Nothing is collected by the developer or any third party.
- Nothing is transmitted off your computer. The extension loads no remote code and contacts no website, including the developer's.
- Nothing is sold, rented, shared or used for advertising, profiling, credit or any purpose unrelated to closing duplicate tabs.
- Settings are not synced between devices.

## Permissions and why they are needed

- `tabs`: read tab URLs and titles to find duplicates, close them, and switch to the existing tab.
- `storage`: keep your settings and, if you choose, the closed tab history.
- `contextMenus`: the right-click menu on the toolbar icon.
- `alarms`: a once-a-minute clean-up of expired history entries.

No website (host) access is requested.

## Your control

Open the history page to clear closed tab history, use Reset on the settings page, or remove the extension to delete everything it stored. Browsers also let you clear extension data from their own settings.

## Children

The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
